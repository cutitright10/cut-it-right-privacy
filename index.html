本政策說明《一刀分對》（以下稱「本遊戲」）及本政策頁面的資料處理方式。Android、iOS 與測試版本的功能可能不同；廣告與付款相關處理僅在具備並使用該功能的版本發生。遊戲不要求建立遊戲帳號，也沒有自行營運的雲端進度同步服務。

本遊戲在裝置上保存關卡進度、星數、收藏、刀具、糖果、挑戰券、活動紀錄、廣告獎勵額度、付費權益、交易去重紀錄，以及語言、音效、震動與介面偏好，用於運作遊戲、保留進度和避免重複發獎。這些存檔不會由遊戲程式自動上傳到開發者伺服器；裝置或作業系統的備份另依您的系統設定運作。

2. 可選的本機分析

本機分析預設關閉。啟用後，遊戲會在裝置上記錄遊玩事件、關卡、切割參數、結果及獎勵或購買操作結果，用於本機遊玩分析。紀錄最多保留最近 1,000 筆，不自動傳送給開發者；您可在設定中關閉分析或清除分析紀錄。此開關不控制第三方廣告 SDK 的資料處理。

3. 廣告與隱私選擇

具備原生廣告的版本使用 Google AdMob 提供自願觀看的獎勵廣告。Google 及其廣告服務合作方可能處理 IP 位址及推估概略位置、裝置或應用程式識別資訊、廣告觀看與互動、當機及效能診斷等資料，用於廣告提供、衡量、安全與防詐騙。實際處理取決於 SDK 版本、受眾設定、裝置權限、所在地及隱私選擇；非個人化廣告仍可能需要處理這些資料。

原生廣告版本使用 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）管理適用的隱私選擇；若 SDK 要求提供隱私選項入口，您可從遊戲設定開啟並變更選擇。遊戲本身不要求存取精確位置、聯絡人、相機或麥克風，也不自行建立跨應用程式追蹤服務。iOS 廣告尚未啟用的版本不會透過 iOS 廣告 SDK 請求廣告；啟用後應以該版本的實際 SDK 設定與系統權限為準。

可購買的版本透過 Apple App Store／StoreKit 或 Google Play／Play Billing 處理付款。商店處理帳號、付款及訂單資料；遊戲取得商品、交易識別或購買憑證、交易狀態與權益資訊，以驗證購買、發放內容、防止重複發貨及恢復可恢復的永久商品。遊戲不直接取得您的信用卡號、商店密碼或完整付款帳戶資料。恢復購買不等於恢復遊戲進度或跨平台同步。

本遊戲供兒童與成人遊玩，不要求填寫生日或年齡，不設玩家聊天或公開個人檔案。現行 Android 原生廣告採兒童保護待遇、G 級廣告內容設定，並停用廣告識別碼權限及 publisher first-party ID；這些措施不表示第三方完全不處理資料。iOS 廣告啟用前須確認相應保護設定。家長可透過商店與裝置家長控制管理購買；若認為兒童向我們提供了個人資料，可透過上述信箱聯絡。

This policy covers 一刀分對 (the “Game”) and this policy page. Features vary between Android, iOS and test builds. The Game saves progress, stars, collections, knives, candy, tickets, event records, reward quotas, paid entitlements, transaction deduplication records and preferences locally. These support gameplay, saved progress and correct reward delivery. The game code does not automatically upload these saves to a developer server. Operating-system or device backups follow your system settings.

2. Optional local analytics

Local analytics are off by default. When enabled, gameplay events, levels, cut parameters, results and reward or purchase operation outcomes are recorded on your device for local gameplay analysis. Up to the latest 1,000 events are retained and are not automatically sent to the developer. You can disable analytics or clear the log in Settings. This switch does not control third-party advertising SDK processing.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

Native-ad builds use Google AdMob for voluntary rewarded ads. Google and its advertising service partners may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash or performance diagnostics for ad delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Processing depends on the SDK version, audience settings, permissions, region and privacy choices. Non-personalized ads may still involve such processing.

Native-ad builds use Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for applicable privacy choices. When the SDK requires a privacy-options entry point, you can open it from Settings and change your choices. The Game itself does not request precise location, contacts, camera or microphone access or operate its own cross-app tracking service. iOS builds without enabled native ads do not request ads through an iOS ad SDK. Builds that enable them must follow their actual SDK configuration and system permissions.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases, where available, use Apple App Store / StoreKit or Google Play / Play Billing. Stores handle accounts, payments and orders. The Game receives product information, transaction identifiers or purchase receipts, transaction status and entitlements to verify purchases, deliver content, prevent duplicate delivery and restore eligible permanent products. It does not directly receive card numbers, store passwords or full payment-account details. Purchase restoration does not restore game progress or synchronize across platforms.

5. Children and families

The Game is intended for children and adults. It does not ask for birth dates or ages and provides no player chat or public profiles. Current Android native ads use child-directed treatment and a G content rating, with advertising-ID permissions and publisher first-party ID disabled. These safeguards do not mean that third parties process no data. Corresponding safeguards must be verified before iOS ads are enabled. Parents can manage purchases through store and device parental controls and contact us if they believe a child has provided personal data to us.

6. Contact, sharing and international processing

If you contact us, we process the email address, messages and attachments you provide to answer questions, handle privacy requests or troubleshoot. Do not send passwords, full payment details or unnecessary child data. We do not automatically provide local saves to advertising services or independently sell local gameplay records. Advertising, store, email and hosting providers process data under their policies; disclosure to competent authorities may occur when legally required. Providers may process data outside your country.

7. Retention, security and rights

Local data remain until cleared or removed under your device settings. iOS “Offload App” may retain data, and system backups may retain copies. Deleting local data may lose progress or consumable content and does not delete records held by stores or Google. Contact information is retained as needed to resolve requests, follow up and meet legal obligations. Provider records follow their retention policies. We take reasonable measures to protect data under our control, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent through the contact above, or complain to a local authority. We cannot directly access device-only data or delete data independently held by third parties; we will explain available options.

This page contains no ads, analytics scripts, forms or external fonts. If hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process IP addresses and access logs to provide and secure the website; see its policy below. Linked websites have their own policies. We update the effective date when features, SDKs or data uses change, and provide further notice or obtain consent where required by law.
